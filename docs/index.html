Frequently asked questions

Why isn't this on the App Store?

VoiceCapture is distributed directly through Apple's TestFlight program rather than the public App Store. You'll install it the same way beta testers do — no developer mode, no jailbreak, just the free TestFlight app from Apple.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get a confirmation page immediately, and a TestFlight invite sent to your email within 24 hours. From there, installing takes about a minute.

Which devices does this work on?

iPhone 13 and above.

Is my voice data sent anywhere?

Transcription happens fully on your device. Only the resulting text is sent off-device, and only to extract tasks — never your raw audio.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time purchase. You own it, no recurring charges.
