Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Voice recordings and transcripts

VoiceCapture transcribes your recordings entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your raw audio is never uploaded anywhere. Only the resulting text transcript is sent off-device, and only for the purpose of extracting tasks and a summary — it is not stored on our servers beyond processing that single request, and it is never used to train any model.

Purchase information

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We receive your email address so we can send your TestFlight invite. We do not receive or store your card details — Lemon Squeezy handles that directly.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@voicecaptureapp.com.
